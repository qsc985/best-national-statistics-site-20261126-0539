Japan Stat Atlas — an independent site built from public statistics. Population, labour and prices: Statistics Bureau of Japan. National accounts: Cabinet Office. Trade: Ministry of Finance. Wages and life expectancy: Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare. GDP in dollars: IMF. Not affiliated with any government office.

Figures are labelled with their source and year on every page; the Data & Sources page lists them all and offers the dataset as a CSV.
